# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect code contributors, project maintainers, and community members to show respect to one another.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive in discussions
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being patient with others who are learning
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Acknowledging and giving credit to others' work
- Being professional in all interactions

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation
- Sexist, racist, or otherwise objectionable comments
- Personal attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Publishing private information without consent
- Spamming or trolling
- Any other conduct inappropriate for a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. Those who violate this Code may be banned from the community at the discretion of the maintainers.
